AKRON Saberjet Nozzle

The ultimate CAFS nozzle, the SaberJet is truly multi-purpose. The Dual Shutoff (DSO) allows you to flow both solid bore and fog stream at the same time.

AKRON Turbojet Nozzle

The Turbojet is a constant gallonage nozzle (flow remains the same in all patterns) with multiple flow settings for operator flow control. Use the Turbojet one time and see why it's the nozzle of choice by firefighters worldwide.

AKRON ProVenger Nozzle

The ProVenger integrates quality stream performance with basic flow and pressure options. Take this selectable gallonage handline nozzle from straight stream to wide fog with just a quarter turn bumper adjustment.
