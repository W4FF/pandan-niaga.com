Ladder

All of our TX family complies with the requirements of BS EN 1147 and are three PERSON RESCUE rated. In particular another feature within the TX family is the reduced width of both the ladder and the stabiliser bar as seen in the ASL12m TX ladder, which allows for the normal three beam gantry side by side layout on a flat top box rear body vehicle.
