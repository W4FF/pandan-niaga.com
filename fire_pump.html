Compact and lightweight portable fire pump with a rated performance of 800l/min at 5 bar of pressure with a 3 metre lift.. Powered by 18HP Briggs and Stratton engine, this pump uses twin discharge valves and is available with exhaust priming system, electric or manual starting.

GODIVA Blaze Warrrior 10/10

Meet the Blaze Warrior 10/10 — the latest breakthrough from Godiva, engineered for the evolving demands of today’s firefighting and emergency services. This ultra-lightweight, high performance portable pump delivers an impressive 1000 L/min at 10 bar, fully compliant with EN1028 / PFPN10-1000 standards — all in a compact design that’s significantly smaller, lighter and more compact than comparable units. Built for rapid deployment and reliable operation in high-pressure situations, the Blaze Warrior 10/10 features an advanced cooling system for uninterrupted performance, together with optimised ergonomics for effortless transport and storage.
