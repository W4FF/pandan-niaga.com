Pandan Niaga Sdn. Bhd.

Introduction

Pandan Niaga Sdn. Bhd. (PNSB) is a company establish on 5th November 1996. The Company had commenced its business operation in supplying office supplies and equipment to Government and SemiGovernment agencies in November 1996.

PNSB is a wholly owned by bumiputras and have been registered with the Ministry of Finance since March 2001.

PNSB has diversified its business activities to supplying fire, rescue and safety equipment since 1996 to the Government as well as the Private Sector and and is registered with Ministry of Finance under the following categories:-

  • Municipal and Military Fire and Rescues Services
  • Airport and Port Fire and Rescue Services
  • Fire Fighting Equipment and System
  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) and Related Items
