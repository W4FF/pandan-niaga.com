Universal®F3 Green 3%-3%

Universal®F3 Green 3%-3% Alcohol Resistant Synthetic Fluorine Free Foam (AR-SFFF) concentrate produces an excellent quality firefighting foam blanket designed to protect Class B hazards. The concentrate is formulated for use as a 3% solution on both hydrocarbon and polar solvent fuel fires and produces a vapor-sealing foam blanket that quickly spreads over the fuel surface to help deliver rapid control and extinguishment.

Foam Chamber

Foam makers and chambers are used to protect open- or floating-roof fuel storage tanks as well as diked spill areas. These devices are side-mounted on the tank interior and apply expanded foam directly onto the flammable liquid surface, suppressing fire and fuel vapors.

Remote Control Manual Monitor

Remote control monitors provide maximum flexibility for long-range firefighting. They can be placed near the hazard, with the foam spray pattern, elevation, and rotation adjusted remotely. This capability ensures effective stream delivery while reducing the risk to responders.
